Tower Bridge $

★ 4.7 · Attraction · Tower Bridge Gateway, Sacramento, CA 95814

American River Bike Trail (Jedediah Smith Memorial Trail) $

★ 4.8 · Parks & Trails · Discovery Park, 1000 Garden Hwy, Sacramento, CA 95833

32 miles of paved trail from the river confluence at Discovery Park to Folsom Lake — one of the longest purpose-built bike paths in the country. No rental kiosk at the trailhead itself; rent downtown at City Bicycle Works and pedal out.

Hours: Sun 12am–12pm · Mon 12am–12pm · Tue 12am–12pm · Wed 12am–12pm · Thu 12am–12pm · Fri 12am–12pm · Sat 12am–12pm
