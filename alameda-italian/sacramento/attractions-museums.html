California State Railroad Museum $

★ 4.8 · Attraction · 111 I St, Sacramento, CA 95814

Sacramento History Museum & Underground Tours $

★ 4.5 · Attraction · 101 I St, Sacramento, CA 95814

Sacramento's civic story from Gold Rush to statehood, plus the city's only underground tour through the hollow sidewalks built when downtown raised itself out of flood mud. The Underground Tour runs a limited seasonal window (June 2026) — reserve ahead, only 20 people per tour.

Crocker Art Museum $

★ 4.7 · Attraction · 216 O St, Sacramento, CA 95814
