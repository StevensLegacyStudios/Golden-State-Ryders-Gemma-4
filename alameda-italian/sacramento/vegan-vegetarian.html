Chef Burma $

★ 4.7 · Burmese · 1020 16th St, Ste 10, Sacramento, CA 95814

Sacramento's only Burmese restaurant and one of Midtown's most-loved newer openings — the tea leaf salad and pumpkin curry get name-checked constantly, and the cocktail list punches well above what you'd expect for the category.

Giò Chả Đức Hương (Duc Huong Sandwiches) $

Pushkin's Bakery $

★ 4.6 · Bakery · 1820 29th St, Sacramento, CA 95816

Sacramento's first fully gluten- and dairy-free bakery, and a Midtown standout even for people who eat gluten. Cupcakes, scones, and sandwiches so good you'd never guess — celiac-safe and genuinely delicious.

Queen Sheba Ethiopian Cuisine $

★ 4.3 · Ethiopian · 1704 Broadway, Sacramento, CA 95818

Revolution Winery & Kitchen $

★ 4.4 · Wine Bar · 2831 S St, Sacramento, CA 95816

Sacramento's only fully operational urban winery — the wine is made right there on S St — paired with an almost entirely plant-based, seasonal kitchen. Approachable pours, low-key patio, and a genuinely vegan-friendly menu. Closed Mondays.
